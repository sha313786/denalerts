ABOUT DENALERTS

Built to Give 100% Back to Streamers

We engineered DenAlerts to eliminate unfair 30% platform cuts and escrow holding periods. 100% of viewer donations land directly in your personal bank account via NPCI UPI with instant 60fps OBS overlay triggers.

The Problem Traditional Platforms

Unfair Cuts & 30-Day Escrow Holds

Most gaming stream donation tools were designed for Western payment processors (PayPal/Stripe) that don't fit India's instant UPI ecosystem.

  • YouTube takes a hefty 30% cut of every Super Chat and withholds payouts until Net-30.
  • Third-party aggregators require business merchant accounts, GSTIN, and complex paperwork.
  • Streamers risk funds getting frozen inside wallet balances without legal recourse.
The DenAlerts Architecture Zero Escrow

Direct P2P NPCI Bank Settlement

DenAlerts acts purely as a real-time notification bridge — we never touch, escrow, or route your money.

  • Viewers scan your personal UPI QR code directly on stream via PhonePe, GPay, or Paytm.
  • 100% of the funds transfer peer-to-peer into your savings account in under 1 second.
  • Our Android companion app catches the bank credit notification and triggers the OBS alert in <180ms.
ENGINEERING INTEGRITY

How DenAlerts Protects Broadcasters

Engineered with uncompromising standards for streamer privacy, zero credential exposure, and rapid maintenance.

01

Zero Escrow Risk

Because money moves bank-to-bank through official NPCI UPI rails, DenAlerts cannot freeze, hold, or deduct from your income. You retain 100% financial custody.

02

Zero Banking Credentials

We never request, access, or store banking logins, debit cards, OTPs, or UPI PINs. The companion app only listens to incoming credit notification text.

03

Active Developer Maintenance

Whenever GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm modify their Android notification layouts, SRB STUDIOS rolls out immediate APK updates to ensure uninterrupted broadcasts.

Compliance & Fair Play Commitment

Built Strictly as Broadcaster Infrastructure

DenAlerts is designed strictly to enhance audience engagement and broadcast aesthetics during live streams. We operate fully within the guidelines of major streaming platforms:

  • No Artificial Engagement: DenAlerts does not sell, generate, or manipulate views, likes, comments, subscribers, or watch hours.
  • Transparent Overlays: Alerts are rendered locally inside your own OBS Studio / Streamlabs canvas via standard HTML5 browser source technology.
  • Independent Software: DenAlerts is an independent product developed by SRB STUDIOS and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NPCI, Google, PhonePe, or Paytm.
