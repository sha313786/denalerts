Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about setting up DenAlerts on your gaming stream.

How does DenAlerts offer 0% commission?
Traditional platforms sit in the middle of transactions, hold funds in an escrow wallet, and deduct 5% to 30%. DenAlerts is a direct notification bridge: your viewers scan your personal UPI QR code directly. The money moves peer-to-peer into your personal bank account, and our companion app detects the transaction to fire the alert on OBS.
Will my personal messages or bank transfers show on stream?
No! The Android companion app has a dedicated Live Stream Mode toggle. When you're off-stream, it completely ignores notifications. Additionally, our parser only recognizes incoming credits and ignores all outgoing debit messages and personal transactions.
Do I need a commercial GST or merchant account?
Not at all. You can use your ordinary personal UPI ID (e.g. from Google Pay, PhonePe, or BHIM) that is linked to your normal savings bank account. No business registration or KYC approval is required.
How do I add the alerts to OBS Studio?
In your DenAlerts Cockpit, you get a unique Overlay URL. In OBS Studio, simply click + > Browser Source, paste the URL, set the resolution to 1920x1080, and check "Shutdown source when not visible". It works instantly!
Can I use DenAlerts with Streamlabs Desktop or Prism Live?
Yes! DenAlerts works seamlessly with OBS Studio, Streamlabs Desktop, Prism Live Studio, vMix, and any broadcasting software that supports standard HTML/CSS browser sources.
