Architecture

How DenAlerts Works

A peer-to-peer notification relay designed specifically for streamers. No intermediate wallets. No payout schedules.

STEP 01

Add Browser Source

Copy your unique 1920x1080 transparent browser source overlay URL directly into OBS Studio, Streamlabs, or vMix. The canvas remains completely invisible until an alert arrives.

STEP 02

Pair Mobile App

Install the lightweight DenAlerts Android APK on the phone that receives your UPI notifications. Enter your 6-digit PIN and grant read access to incoming payment notifications.

STEP 03

Instant Live Announcements

When a viewer scans your personal UPI QR code, the mobile app intercepts the transaction, extracts the donor's name, and relays it to your stream in milliseconds with native voice TTS.
