Privacy & Data Security Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Published by SRB STUDIOS (alerts.denbooks.in)

1. Zero Banking Credential Philosophy

DenAlerts is engineered with strict financial privacy in mind. We NEVER ask for, record, process, or store:

  • Bank account passwords or net banking logins.
  • Debit or credit card numbers, CVVs, or expiration dates.
  • One-Time Passwords (OTPs) or UPI PINs.

2. Android Companion App Notification Listener

The DenAlerts Android application uses the standard Android NotificationListenerService API strictly to detect incoming UPI payment credit notifications.

  • Credit-Only Parsing: The application only parses notifications from verified UPI payment apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, Cred) containing positive credit terms (e.g., "received", "credited").
  • Personal Privacy: Private chat apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS messages, personal emails), debit transactions, account balances, and unrelated notifications are strictly ignored and never transmitted.
  • Off-Stream Switch: When you turn off "Live Stream Mode" in the app, the notification listener is paused immediately.

3. Data Transmission & Encryption

All communication between your Android device, the Cockpit Dashboard, and OBS overlay widgets is secured over modern TLS/HTTPS encryption. Data is tagged strictly with your unique 6-digit Pairing PIN and Streamer Key.

4. Data Retention

Recent donation records displayed in the Cockpit are retained temporarily in volatile session storage for your active broadcast summary. Broadcasters can clear history at any time using the "Clear Feed" button in the Dashboard.

5. Inquiries & Privacy Contact

If you have any questions or data concerns, contact our privacy officer directly at support@denbooks.in.
