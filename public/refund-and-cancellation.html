Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Product developed by SRB STUDIOS (denbooks.in)

1. Scope & Nature of Services

DenAlerts is a specialized live-streaming utility and notification bridge. It enables creators to display visual and auditory alerts (including custom sounds and regional Indian Text-to-Speech) on OBS Studio, Streamlabs, and browser sources when verified UPI payments arrive on the creator's Android mobile device.

Important Clarification: DenAlerts is NOT a payment gateway, payment aggregator, financial institution, or escrow wallet. DenAlerts NEVER handles, holds, or deducts viewer donations. All viewer payments settle 100% peer-to-peer (P2P) directly into the creator's personal bank account via the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) UPI network.

2. Free Forever Tier

DenAlerts provides a Free Tier offering 50 real-time alerts per month with 0% platform commission. No payment information or credit card is required to sign up or use the Free Tier. Because the service is free of charge, no refunds apply to the free tier.

3. Creator Pro Digital Subscription (₹99 / month)

Creator Pro is an instantly delivered digital SaaS subscription providing unlimited alerts, custom sound upload capability, and synthetic Indian voice speech synthesis.

  • No Lock-in / Cancel Anytime: You may cancel your Creator Pro recurring subscription at any time with a single click from the Creator Cockpit or by notifying our support team.
  • Access Post-Cancellation: If you cancel your subscription, your Pro features remain fully accessible until the conclusion of your current 30-day paid billing cycle. You will not be billed again.
  • Zero Cancellation Surcharges: There are no cancellation fees, penalty fees, or termination surcharges.

4. 7-Day Technical Resolution & Refund Guarantee

We pride ourselves on active development and compatibility maintenance with major Indian UPI applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM UPI).

  • If an Android operating system update or a third-party UPI app redesign impairs alert detection on your compatible Android device, and our developer team cannot provide a working software patch or guidance within 7 business days of your reported issue, you are eligible for a 100% full refund of your most recent Creator Pro monthly subscription charge.
  • To request a technical refund, simply email support@denbooks.in or message the developer in the official DenAlerts Discord with your registered account email and brief details of the issue.

5. Viewer Donations & Tip Disputes

Because all donations move directly from the viewer's UPI app to the streamer's bank account via P2P UPI:

  • DenAlerts does not possess or hold any viewer donations and has no technical ability to reverse or refund bank-to-bank UPI transactions.
  • Any requests for tip refunds or accidental transfer disputes must be handled directly between the sender and the recipient streamer.

6. Compliance & Integrity Policy

DenAlerts does not provide, sell, or facilitate subscriber, follower, view, or watch-hour growth services. Users agree not to utilize DenAlerts for deceptive practices or simulated fraudulent transaction notifications on live streams.
