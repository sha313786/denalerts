Step-by-Step Setup Checklist

Follow these 5 simple steps to get your live UPI alerts up and running

01

Create Free Account

Sign in to the DenAlerts Cockpit using your email or Google account to generate your private Stream Token and unique OBS overlay URL.

  • Instant activation with zero phone number requirement
  • Copy your unique 1080p Browser Source URL
Open Cockpit
02

Download Companion APK

Download and install the official DenAlerts Android companion app directly onto the phone where you receive your Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm UPI notifications.

  • Lightweight (~6 MB), zero battery drain
  • No root access or banking passwords required
Download APK
03

Grant Permissions & Pair PIN

Open the app, allow Notification Access so it can detect incoming UPI credit alerts, and enter the 6-digit PIN shown on your Cockpit screen.

  • App displays green "Connected • Cloud Synced" indicator
  • Toggle Live Stream Mode ON when broadcasting
✓ Sub-second cloud pairing
04

Add Browser Source in OBS

In OBS Studio or Streamlabs, add a new Browser Source, paste your overlay URL, set resolution to 1920x1080, and check "Shutdown source when not visible".

  • Supports OBS Studio, Streamlabs, Prism Live & vMix
  • Transparent 60 FPS hardware-accelerated canvas
Resolution: 1920 × 1080
05

Simulate Test Alert & Go Live!

Head to the Alert Simulator in Cockpit and click "Simulate Superchat". You will hear the sound chime and Indian voice TTS announce the donor name live on stream!

  • Customizable voice models and sound chimes
  • 100% peer-to-peer donation settlement direct to bank
Launch Alert Simulator
