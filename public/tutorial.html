STEP-BY-STEP

5 Easy Steps to Go Live

STEP 01

Register & Launch Creator Cockpit

Visit alerts.denbooks.in/login. Sign in to your dashboard to get your persistent Overlay URL and unique 6-digit companion PIN.

STEP 02

Download & Pair Android Companion APK

Download the official DenAlerts APK (v2.5) on the Android phone where you receive your UPI payments. Enter your 6-digit PIN and tap "Connect".

STEP 03

Add Browser Source into OBS Studio

In OBS Studio, click + > Browser in your Sources pane. Name it "DenAlerts", paste your Overlay URL, set resolution to 1920x1080, and check "Shutdown source when not visible".

STEP 04

Display Your Personal UPI QR on Stream

Place your personal Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm QR code image in your stream scene corner. Viewers scan directly to pay straight into your bank.

STEP 05

Toggle Live Stream Mode ON & Go Live!

Whenever you start broadcasting, toggle Live Stream Mode ON in the Android app. Incoming tips will fire with instant animated graphics and Indian voice TTS!
